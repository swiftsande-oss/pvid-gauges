What does “provisional” data mean?

This website harvests data from USGS and OSE gauge websites that are marked “provisional.” At the end of each year, those agencies may adjust the data to account for calibration changes, known instrument problems, debris stuck in gauges, and the like — but the PVID Gauge Explorer will not be updated with such changes. If a gauge sends no reading to OSE for a long time, the Gauge Explorer assumes a linear interpolation between readings.

What are the units of water flow?

CFS = cubic foot per second. AF = acre-foot = 43,560 cubic feet. If one CFS flows steadily for 24 hours, a total of 1.98 acre-feet has passed.

How are the data manipulated in the graph?

  • Data adjustments = None and Graph = Flow (CFS) shows exactly the data harvested from USGS and OSE.
  • Data adjustments = Adjust: hover your mouse over this button in the explorer to see what it does.
  • Graph = Total (AF) adds up each gauge’s CFS during the displayed time interval, showing the accumulated total as a function of time. A linear interpolation between gauge readings is used. A long interval of “no reading” of a gauge is shown as a dashed line on the graph.

None of these manipulations changes the CFS table itself — only how the data are displayed.
