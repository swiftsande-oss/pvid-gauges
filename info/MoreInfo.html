More information

What does “provisional” data mean?

The Gauge Explorer harvests data from USGS and OSE gauge websites that are marked “provisional.” At the end of each year, USGS and OSE may adjust their data to account for calibration changes, known instrument problems, debris stuck in gauges, and the like — but the PVID Gauge Explorer will not be updated with such changes.

Each harvest gets only a few days of data. The Gauge Explorer refreshes itself with anything new since the last harvest. If a gauge originally sent no reading to OSE for a long time, the Gauge Explorer assumes a linear interpolation between those readings, and never goes back to USGS or OSE to see if the blank data were later filled in.

How often is the data refreshed?

Now, running on GitHub (a free service), we have asked for a refresh every 20 minutes. But GitHub often gets overloaded and slows down, sometimes for hours. If this becomes too annoying, we'll change to a paid service for better reliability.

What are the units of water flow?

CFS = cubic foot per second.

AF = acre-foot = 43,560 cubic feet. If one CFS flows steadily for 24 hours, a total of 1.98 acre-feet has passed.

How are the data manipulated in the graph?

  • Data adjustments = None and Graph = Flow (CFS) shows exactly the data harvested from USGS and OSE.
  • Data adjustments = Adjust: Hover your mouse over this button in the Gauge Explorer to see what it does.
  • Graph = Total (AF) adds up each gauge’s CFS during the displayed time interval, showing the accumulated total as a function of time. A linear interpolation between gauge readings is used. A long interval of “no reading” of a gauge is shown as a dashed line on the Total(AF) graph.
  • None of the graphical data manipulations changes the Gauge Explorer's CFS table itself — only how the data are displayed.

For TotalAF/acre, are the "irrigable acres" accurate?

No, not yet. A work in progress. In the future, maybe we should use TBI reports for the non-Pueblo acequias, and increase Nambe Pueblo's number to include acres to be irrigated from surface water instead of metered wells. For now, hover your mouse over the TotalAF/acre button to see what we're using.
